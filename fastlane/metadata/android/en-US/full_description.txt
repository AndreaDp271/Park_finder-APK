ParkMap finds where to park using OpenStreetMap data: paid, free and disc-zone parking, disabled bays, charging stations, motorcycle and bicycle parking and parking meters.

• Map with filters (free now, disc, disabled, EV charging, motorcycle, bicycle) and Standard, Humanitarian, Cycle, Topographic, Gray, Night and Satellite styles
• Payment times interpreted: "free now" and an estimated cost for the duration you choose
• Search an address and see nearby parking with walking distance
• My car: save where you parked, with a note, photo, expiry reminder, history and walking directions back to it
• Automatic save when the phone disconnects from the car's Bluetooth
• "My car" widget and icon shortcuts
• Favourites and error reports sent as OpenStreetMap notes
• Italian and English, light and dark theme, wallpaper colours (Android 12+)
• No Google services, no ads, no account

A web version for computers and iPhone is also available: https://andreadp271.github.io/Park_finder/
